# Template D Question Templates

Use this exact Template D question wording. Replace `[a certain video span]` with the target video span when generating an item. Replace `[three feedback points]` with the three specific named points (for example "economy of motion, flow of operation, and instrument/needle handling") -- never leave this generic. A question that says "these three feedback points" without ever naming them is not self-contained: a reader must be able to understand and attempt to answer the question from its text alone, with no hidden context.

## Template D1: Coaching Feedback

If you were the supervising surgeon, based only on the events occurring during [a certain video span], what feedback would you give?

## Template D2: Feedback With Priority

Given the trainee's performance during [a certain video span], among [three feedback points], which area should the trainee improve first and why?

## Template D3: Corrective Action

During [a certain video span], among [three feedback points], which is most urgent, and what should the trainee do immediately to address it?

## Template D4: Practice Recommendation

Among [three feedback points], what simulation drill should the trainee practice next to strengthen the weakest area?

## Template D5: Positive Reinforcement

Based on this trainee's performance, what is their strongest technical skill, and why does it matter?
