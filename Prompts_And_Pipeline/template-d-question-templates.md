# Template D Question Templates

Use this exact Template D question wording. Replace `[a certain video span]` with the target video span when generating an item.

## Template D1: Coaching Feedback

If you were the supervising surgeon, based only on the events occurring within this video segment, what feedback would you give at [a certain video span]?

## Template D2: Feedback With Priority

Given the trainee's performance in [a certain video span], among these three feedback points, which area should the trainee improve first?

## Template D3: Corrective Action

What should the trainee do immediately to improve the situation in [a certain video span]? Prioritize one most urgent among these three feedback points.

## Template D4: Practice Recommendation

Based on the observed weakness among the three critical feedback points, what simulation drill should the trainee practice next?

## Template D5: Positive Reinforcement

Identify the trainee’s strongest technical behavior in this clip, and explain why it is good technique.
