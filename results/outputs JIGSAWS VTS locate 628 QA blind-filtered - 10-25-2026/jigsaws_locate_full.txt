  order, a gesture performed once in the trial or once in its throw or named
  by its occurrence, an error that is the only one of its type or named by its
  occurrence, a return to an action right after starting the next, the longest
  throw, or the single step that runs furthest over the time the strongest
  trials take; the two extremes only by a clear margin over the next.
  Questions a guess from the text alone locates at IoU 0.3 or better are left
  out (plan_locate_qa.py). JIGSAWS labels no phases, so a throw (a stitch, a
  pass through a ring, a throw of the knot) is cut from the gesture
  transcription, and only in trials with the number of throws the task calls
  for. The errors are the UVA-DSA consensus labels, which cover Suturing and
  Needle Passing only, and carry no grade; the worst is therefore the longest
  overrun, not the most serious error. Knot Tying recordings have a median of
  45 s, so few of its stretches are under 20% of the video. The generating
  model never saw video.
