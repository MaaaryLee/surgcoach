# Template Questions (B, C, D)

Canonical question wording for Templates B (Safety / Danger Detection QA), C (Skill Assessment QA), and D (Coaching Feedback QA). Copy the selected template's question text exactly and only replace the bracketed placeholders described below -- never leave a placeholder generic or unresolved, and never invent extra wording around the copied text.

For Template D: replace `[a certain video span]` with the target video span when generating an item. Replace `[three feedback points]` with the three specific named points (for example "economy of motion, flow of operation, and instrument/needle handling") -- never leave this generic. A question that says "these three feedback points" without ever naming them is not self-contained: a reader must be able to understand and attempt to answer the question from its text alone, with no hidden context.

## Template B1: Dangerous Frame Critique

At timestamp [t], identify the most urgent patient-safety concern visible in the operative field. Answer with: visible evidence, anatomical structure at risk, mechanism of possible injury, clinical consequence, severity level, and immediate corrective action.

## Template B2: Is It Safe To Proceed?

Based on the frame at [t], is it medically safe to proceed with [action]? Answer yes/no, including anatomical exposure, tissue plane, hemostasis, instrument position, and energy safety uncertain and justify using visible evidence.

## Template B3: Risk Ranking

Rank the visible intraoperative risks from highest to lowest clinical priority: uncontrolled bleeding / vascular injury, bile duct or bowel injury, thermal injury, wrong dissection plane, poor visualization, inadequate traction-countertraction, instrument collision, tissue tearing, loss of anatomical orientation, contamination or leakage.

## Template B4: Near-Miss Detection

Does this clip show a near-miss event that could have resulted in patient harm? If yes, classify the near-miss as one of the following: vascular injury, ductal injury, bowel perforation, thermal spread, tissue avulsion, clip misplacement, unsafe dissection plane, poor visualization, or loss of orientation. Then suggest how to prevent progression to actual injury.

## Template B5: Stop Point

At which timestamp should a supervisor pause the trainee to prevent possible patient harm? Explain: what visible event triggered the stop, what injury is being prevented, which structure is at risk, and what corrective coaching should be given.

## Template B6: Cause Of Error

At which timestamp should a supervisor pause the trainee for coaching, and why?

## Template B7: CVS Safety Check

Based on the frame at [t], is it medically safe to proceed with clipping or cutting the cystic duct or artery?

## Template C1: Tissue Handling Score

Rate the trainee's tissue handling in [clip] from 1-5. Provide: score, visible evidence, potential tissue injury risk, and one concrete improvement.

## Template C2: Instrument Handling Score

Rate instrument handling from 1-5. Consider instrument steadiness, tip visibility, precision, angle of approach, wrist control, avoidance of off-screen movement, and prevention of unintended contact with adjacent tissue. Provide: score, key evidence, unsafe movement if present, and corrective suggestion.

## Template C3: Economy Of Motion

Does the trainee use efficient motion in this clip? Identify unnecessary movements and suggest a more efficient alternative.

## Template C4: Bimanual Dexterity

How well does the trainee coordinate both hands? Identify whether one hand is passive, obstructive, or not optimizing exposure.

## Template C5: Depth Perception / Targeting

Does the trainee accurately direct the instrument tip to the intended plane or target? Identify evidence of overshooting, undershooting, repeated correction, unstable targeting, poor depth judgment, or contact with unintended tissue.

## Template C6: Flow Of Operation

Does the trainee demonstrate smooth procedural flow and anticipation of the next operative step?

## Template C7: Autonomy Level

Based on the clip, what level of supervision would the trainee likely require? Classify as: Independent / minimal verbal cueing / repeated verbal cueing / hands-on guidance / supervisor takeover. Justify using visible evidence, including technical control, anatomical awareness, safety risk, ability to correct errors, and whether the trainee can proceed without compromising patient safety.

## Template D1: Coaching Feedback

If you were the supervising surgeon, based only on the events occurring during [a certain video span], what feedback would you give?

## Template D2: Feedback With Priority

Given the trainee's performance during [a certain video span], among [three feedback points], which area should the trainee improve first and why?

## Template D3: Corrective Action

During [a certain video span], among [three feedback points], which is most urgent, and what should the trainee do immediately to address it?

## Template D4: Practice Recommendation

Among [three feedback points], what simulation drill should the trainee practice next to strengthen the weakest area?

## Template D5: Positive Reinforcement

Based on this trainee's performance, what is their strongest technical skill, and why does it matter?
