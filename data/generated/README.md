# Generated QA Datasets

Real, annotation-only QA pairs generated by `scripts/run_annotation_qa_jigsaws.py` on the lab GPU cluster. No frames were sent to the model; every answer is model-written and grounded solely in JIGSAWS annotations. Every record carries full provenance (`model.backend: "real"`, exact prompt, raw model output including thinking, source annotation values, validation status).

| `d_full_suturing_video` | 2026-07-26 | qwen3.6-35b-a3b-iq4xs (local) | Suturing_B001–B005, one unit per whole video | D1–D5 × 5 trials | 25/25 valid |
| `d2_video_3tasks` | 2026-07-26 | qwen3.6-35b-a3b-iq4xs (local) | 5 trials each of Suturing / Knot_Tying / Needle_Passing | D2 × 15 trials | 15/15 valid |

The first two batches cover the same 10 gesture spans, so C and D records align span-for-span.

Granularity differs between batches, and `qa_pairs.jsonl` reflects it:

- **Gesture** (`c_batch_C001`, `d_batch_C001`) — one QA pair per gesture span; `frames` is a frame range and `gesture` is a gesture ID.
- **Video** (`d_full_suturing_video`, `d2_video_3tasks`) — one QA pair per whole trial; `frames` is a timestamp range (`0:03-3:08`, converted at JIGSAWS's 30 fps) and `gesture` reads `whole video (N gestures)`. Every gesture in a trial shares the same GRS scores, so per-gesture coaching questions were near-duplicates of each other; one question per video removes that redundancy.

Notes:

- The two `C001` batches were generated with the prompt stack at commit `26cb846` (before the D1-template simplification and field-name-leakage rule in `7721df0` were live on the cluster). Known cosmetic issues in those batches: some answers name raw GRS field names, and skill-level codes were unglossed (one C7 answer calls level `I` "novice"; `I` = intermediate). Both prompt fixes are in place for later batches, and the skill-level gloss is now passed to the model explicitly.
- The two `2026-07-26` batches were generated locally through Ollama on a 4-bit (IQ4_XS) quantisation rather than full precision on the cluster, at `temperature 0.7` rather than greedy. They have **not** been reproduced at full precision yet. They screen clean under `scripts/check_template_d.py` (0 issues: no raw field names or bare scores in answers, no references to a hidden rubric, self-contained questions, D3 names its priority area, D5 does not oversell a midpoint score as a strength).
- Known open issue in `d_full_suturing_video`: 5 of 25 answers mention anatomy or outcomes the annotations do not contain (`fascia`, `wound margin`, `postoperative`, `healing`, `bleeding`). JIGSAWS carries no anatomy labels, so that is model-supplied clinical framing. Undecided whether it counts as acceptable general teaching context or should be constrained.
- `qa_records.jsonl`: one full-provenance record per line (source of truth); the QA pair itself is in the `qa` field.
- `qa_pairs.jsonl` / `qa_pairs.md`: clean extracted views of just the QA pairs (minimal JSONL for machines, Markdown grouped by template for humans). Regenerate with `python3 scripts/extract_qa_pairs.py <batch>/qa_records.jsonl` after any new batch.
- Use only records with `validation_status: "valid"` downstream (all records in these files are valid).
